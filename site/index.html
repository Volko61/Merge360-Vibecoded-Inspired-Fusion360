02 / Highlights

What's inside.

Every feature is parametric and stays editable. Double-click it in the timeline, change a number and the model rebuilds.

Sketch · Constrain

Sketches that hold their shape.

Draw on any plane or planar face. A built-in solver keeps the geometry where you put it, and closed regions turn into profiles you can extrude.

  • Lines, arcs, splines, ellipses, slots, polygons and text
  • Dimensions plus coincident, tangent, symmetry and nine more constraints
  • Trim, extend, offset, fillet, mirror and patterns
Click to enlarge
Create · Push-pull

Live preview, real solids.

Pick a profile or a face, then drag the arrow or type a value. You see the result before you commit, and join, cut or new body is picked from context.

  • Extrude with taper, revolve, sweep, pipe, loft and coil
  • Holes with counterbore and countersink, threads, patterns and mirror
  • Fillet, chamfer, shell, draft, split and combine
Click to enlarge
Inspect

Look inside the part.

Cut a section through every body with capped faces. Measure lengths, areas and volumes, check interference, and get mass and center of mass from real material densities.

  • Section analysis on any plane or face
  • Measure, interference and center of mass
  • A library of 19 physical materials
Click to enlarge
Document

From model to paper.

The Drawing workspace lays out front, top, right and isometric views in third-angle projection. Hidden lines are dashed, and scale, dimensions and the title block are filled in for you.

  • A3 sheet with an automatic standard scale
  • True silhouettes of curved faces
  • SVG export for plotting or a CAM workflow

03 / Toolset

Real tools for real parts.

Beyond the highlights, the full toolset covers assemblies, construction geometry, parameters and file exchange.

01History

Feature timeline

Drag the marker to roll back, suppress features and edit any step. You get undo and redo for everything.

02Params

User parameters

Name your values and use expressions such as width * 2 + 5 mm in any feature.

03Assemble

Components & joints

Rigid, revolute and slider joints with live motion. Hide or activate components from the browser.

04Construct

Construction planes

Offset, at an angle, midplane or through three points. Sketch on faces of any orientation.

05Insert

Mesh & DXF import

Bring in STL meshes as bodies that take part in booleans, and DXF drawings as editable sketches.

06Exchange

STEP & STL export

Send exact B-rep geometry to other CAD tools, or meshes straight to your slicer.

07Prefs

Your language

English, Français, Deutsch and Español, plus interface scaling, default units and autosave.

05 / Ownership

Stop renting your tools.

No license server or forced update. Your files never pass through someone else's computer.

A

Offline by design

No account, cloud sync or telemetry. Merge360 starts up and works on a plane, in a basement or ten years from now.

B

Open source, MIT

Read the code, fork it, fix it or ship it inside your own product. The whole stack is open, from the geometry kernel to the UI.

C

Files you can read

Designs are saved as plain-text .m360 (RON) with the full parametric history. Exchange with everything else through STEP, STL and DXF.

06 / Under the hood

Rust, top to bottom.

A B-rep kernel built on truck, a GPU renderer on wgpu and an immediate-mode UI on egui. It ships as a single native binary.
