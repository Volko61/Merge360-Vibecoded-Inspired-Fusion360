01 / Toolset

Real tools for real parts.

Every feature is parametric and stays editable. Double-click it in the timeline, change a number and the model rebuilds.

01Sketch

Parametric sketches

Lines, arcs, splines, slots and text, held in place by a built-in constraint solver with dimensions, tangency, symmetry and more.

02Create

Solid modeling

Extrude with taper, revolve, sweep, pipe, loft, hole, coil, patterns and mirror. Every one has live preview and drag handles.

03Modify

Fillet, shell, combine

Fillet, chamfer, shell, split, combine and press-pull. Apply materials and the app computes mass and center of mass.

04History

Feature timeline

Drag the marker to roll back, suppress features or reorder your thinking. You get undo and redo for everything.

05Assemble

Components & joints

Rigid, revolute and slider joints with live motion. Check interference and cut section views with caps.

06Document

Drawings

A3 sheets with front, top, right and isometric views. Hidden lines are dashed, and you get automatic scale and dimensions with SVG export.

07Render

Presentation renders

Environments, soft ground shadows and optional edges. Export PNG images at up to 4K.

03 / Ownership

Stop renting your tools.

No license server or forced update. Your files never pass through someone else's computer.

A

Offline by design

No account, cloud sync or telemetry. Merge360 starts up and works on a plane, in a basement or ten years from now.

B

Open source, MIT

Read the code, fork it, fix it or ship it inside your own product. The whole stack is open, from the geometry kernel to the UI.

C

Files you can read

Designs are saved as plain-text .m360 (RON) with the full parametric history. Exchange with everything else through STEP, STL and DXF.

04 / Under the hood

Rust, top to bottom.

A B-rep kernel built on truck, a GPU renderer on wgpu and an immediate-mode UI on egui. It ships as a single native binary.
